Pandit Bano collects no personal data. There are no accounts, cookies, analytics, advertisements or third-party requests. Your choices on this site — language, theme, transliteration, voice speed, checklist ticks, quiz progress, the name and gotra typed into the sankalpa example, and pages saved for offline use — are stored only in your own browser and never sent anywhere; clearing this site's data in your browser removes them. The site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/): GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security under [its own privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Corrections are received through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Sudish007/panditbano/issues), where GitHub's terms apply.

# Exactly what the browser stores

Every item lives in the browser's local storage ([localStorage](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/API/Window/localStorage)) on the same device, and the code that writes it can be read in the site's [open source](https://github.com/Sudish007/panditbano/tree/main/assets/js). Nothing goes to a server. The table at the foot of this page lists every key the site writes, and the list is complete. Only one of them is something you type yourself — the gotra and name in the sankalpa example — and it, too, stays in that browser; you may leave the form empty.

# Offline copies

The site installs a service worker so that pages you have visited, and occasions you chose with "Save offline", open without an internet connection. These copies live in the browser's [cache storage](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/API/CacheStorage) on the same device. Clearing this site's data in the browser settings removes them as well.

# No third parties

- Fonts, images and code all come from this site; nothing is requested from an outside server.
- There is no analytics, no tracking and no social-media button on the site.
- External links (such as [Shubhvela](https://sudish007.github.io/shubhvela/en/), Wikisource or publishers' pages) take you to another site, where that site's own policy applies; every such link carries the outward-arrow marker.

# The legal position, in one line

The site neither collects, stores nor transmits any visitor's personal data; your preferences are written by your browser on your own device. In our understanding, therefore, the site processes no data within the meaning of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP). Nothing will ever be added to the site that changes this position.

Questions go to [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Sudish007/panditbano/issues).

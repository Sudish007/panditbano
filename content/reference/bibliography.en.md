This is the list of the works from which the site's text was taken, against which the order of a rite was checked, or whose name appears anywhere on the site. Every name carries a link to a legal online copy or to the publisher's page. What was taken from which source, and what was not, is on the [Sources](/sources/) page; every mantra also carries its own source line beneath it.

# Primary texts

## Veda and grihya-sutra

- **Shukla Yajurveda — the Vajasaneyi Madhyandina Samhita.** The origin of the site's Vedic mantras. [Forty adhyayas on Sanskrit Wikisource](https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/%E0%A4%B6%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%95%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B2%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A6%E0%A4%83) (both the pada text and the accented text); text and recitation on the [Vedic Heritage Portal](https://vedicheritage.gov.in/samhitas/yajurveda/vajasneyi-madhyandina-samhita/); a comparison text on [TITUS](https://titus.uni-frankfurt.de/texte/etcs/ind/aind/ved/yvw/vs/vs.htm); the scan of the [1929 Nirnaya Sagar Press edition](https://archive.org/details/ShuklaYajurvedaSamhita1929WLSPansikarNirnayaSagarPress).
- **[Paraskara Grihya Sutra](https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/%E0%A4%AA%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%97%E0%A5%83%E0%A4%B9%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%8D).** The household manual of the Shukla Yajurveda and the backbone of the site's procedures. Hermann Oldenberg's English translation of 1886 is in [Sacred Books of the East XXIX](https://archive.org/details/india.history.resource.89728); a reading copy opens chapter by chapter as [Grihya Sutras, Part 1 (sacred-texts)](https://sacred-texts.com/hin/sbe29/index.htm).
- **[Rudrashtadhyayi](https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B7%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9F%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A7%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AF%E0%A5%80).** The text of the Rudrabhishek, compiled from adhyayas of the Samhita. Accent marks compared with [Rudrashtadhyayi sasvara (sanskritdocuments)](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_shiva/rudrAShTAdhyAyIshuklayajurvedIyasasvara.html); the [Rudrashtadhyayi (Gita Press, Hindi)](https://gitapress.org/product/rudrastadhyayee-hindi) edition and its [older scan](https://archive.org/details/rudrashtadhyayi-gita-press-gorakhpur) for the sequence only.
- **Comparison texts for the sandhya and the svastivachana.** [Shukla Yajurvediya Sandhya (sanskritdocuments)](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_veda/shuklayajurvedIyasandhyA.html) and [Svastivachanam (sanskritdocuments)](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_veda/svastivAchanam.html).

## Puranas and kathas

- **The Satyanarayan vrat katha (Sanskrit).** Tradition places it in the Reva Khanda of the Skanda Purana; the five adhyayas stand as chapters 233 to 237 of the [Reva Khanda on Sanskrit Wikisource](https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A6%E0%A4%AA%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A3%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%8D/%E0%A4%96%E0%A4%A3%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A1%E0%A4%83_%E0%A5%AB_%28%E0%A4%85%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%80%E0%A4%96%E0%A4%A3%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A1%E0%A4%83%29/%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BE_%E0%A4%96%E0%A4%A3%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A1%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%8D). Compared with [Satyanarayana vratakatha (sanskritdocuments)](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_vishhnu/satyanArAyaNavratakathA.html), the [GRETIL text of the Revakhanda](http://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/plaintext/sa_skandapurANa-revAkhaNDa-rks.txt) and the scan of a [printed copy from the Jangamwadi Math collection](https://archive.org/details/satyanarayan-vrat-puja-katha-sanskrit-printed-jangamwadi-math-collection). In Hindi, the [Satyanarayan Vrat Katha (Gita Press)](https://gitapress.org/product/sri-satya-narayana-vrata-katha-hindi) for the sequence only.
- **Garuda Purana.** The Sanskrit of the death-rite chapters from the [Pretakanda on Sanskrit Wikisource](https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/%E0%A4%97%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%A1%E0%A4%AA%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A3%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%8D/%E0%A4%AA%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A3%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A1%E0%A4%83_%28%E0%A4%A7%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A3%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A1%E0%A4%83%29), compared with the [GRETIL Garudapurana](http://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/plaintext/sa_garuDapurANa.txt). The "Saroddhara" that is actually read (Naunidhirama's medieval digest in sixteen chapters) is online in English as the [Garuda Purana Saroddhara (Wood and Subrahmanyam)](https://sacred-texts.com/hin/gpu/index.htm) of 1911, public domain, and in Hindi as the [Garud Puran Saroddhar (Gita Press)](https://gitapress.org/product/garud-puran-saroddhar-with-simplified-commentary-or-sacred-hindu-scripture-on-dharma-afterlife-and-liberation-or-language-hindi-or-size-medium-or-code-1416).
- **[Matsya Purana](https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%AA%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A3%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%8D).** The Vastu-shanti chapters 252 to 257, on [Sanskrit Wikisource](https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%AA%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A3%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%8D/%E0%A4%85%E0%A4%A7%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%83_%E0%A5%A8%E0%A5%AB%E0%A5%A8).
- **Durga Saptashati (Markandeya Purana).** The Devi-mahatmya of seven hundred verses with the Kavacha, Argala, Kilaka and Kunjika: [durga700](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_devii/durga700.html) on sanskritdocuments; for the order of the recitation, the [Durga Saptashati (Gita Press, Sanskrit)](https://gitapress.org/product/durga-saptashati-or-language-sanskrit-or-code-876).

# Muhurta and dharmashastra

- **[Muhurta Chintamani](https://archive.org/details/muhurt-chintamani)** — Rama Daivajna, about 1600. The tithi, nakshatra and weekday tables of its chapters on marriage, the house and the samskaras, on which the [Muhurat rules](/reference/muhurat-rules/) page rests.
- **[Dharmasindhu](https://archive.org/details/dharmasindhubykashinathupadhyaya1888nirnaysagarpress_498_a)** — Kashinath Upadhyaya, 1790; the scan of the 1888 Nirnaya Sagar Press edition. The basis of the tithi decisions (pitru paksha, the yearly shraddha, the lunar birthday, the intercalary month, the start of a vrat).
- **[Shubhvela](https://sudish007.github.io/shubhvela/en/)** — the dates of tithis, festivals and muhurats; this site computes nothing, and every "When" section links there. The spelling of Hindi and Bhojpuri terms also comes from its term bank.

# Courses in paurohitya

The official pages of the formal routes named in Level 0 of "Learn" (seen in October 2026; eligibility and fees are stated there):

- [Sampurnanand Sanskrit Vishwavidyalaya](https://ssvvostc.ac.in/karmakand/), Varanasi — Karmakand certificates of three and six months and a one-year Karmakand diploma; Shukla Yajurveda and Paurohitya as Shastri and Acharya subjects.
- [Central Sanskrit University](https://www.csu-agartala.edu.in/pg_diploma_in_karmakanda_and_paurohitya.html) — a one-year P.G. Diploma in Karmakanda and Paurohitya; a certificate course.
- [Shri Lal Bahadur Shastri National Sanskrit University](https://www.slbsrsv.ac.in/academic/part-time-courses/diploma-advance-diploma-courses), New Delhi — Diploma in Paurohitya (Karmkand), one year, after Intermediate; also Acharya and research in Paurohitya.
- [Jagadguru Ramanandacharya Rajasthan Sanskrit University](https://admission.jrrsu.in/), Jaipur — Diploma in Paurohitya Karmakanda; a short course in Gram Paurohitya.
- [Uttarakhand Sanskrit University](http://usvv.ac.in/faculty-veda-jyotish.php), Haridwar — P.G. Diploma in Paurohitya and Karmkand (Jyotish department).
- [Uttar Pradesh Sanskrit Sansthan](https://upsanskritsansthanam.in/en/page/training-scheme), Lucknow — Karmkand training camps and a public registry of Paurohitya teachers.
- [Maharshi Panini Sanskrit evam Vedic Vishwavidyalaya](https://www.mpsvv.ac.in/index.php/en/admission), Ujjain, and [Kameshwar Singh Darbhanga Sanskrit University](https://ksdsu.bihar.gov.in/), Darbhanga — Shastri and Acharya studies; a separate Karmakanda diploma was not confirmed on the official pages.
- [Jnana Prabodhini](https://www.jnanaprabodhini.org/paurohitya/), Pune — paurohitya training for women and men of every community since 1990; the example of open admission.

# Tools and standards

The site is built by its own code on [Node](https://nodejs.org/); the browser checks run in [Playwright](https://playwright.dev/) and the performance checks in [Lighthouse](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/lighthouse/overview). The Roman transliteration of the Devanagari is produced with [sanscript](https://github.com/indic-transliteration/sanscript.js). The typefaces are [Tiro Devanagari Sanskrit](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Tiro+Devanagari+Sanskrit), [Noto Sans Devanagari](https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Sans+Devanagari) and [Noto Sans](https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Sans). The accessibility standard is [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/); language tags follow the [IANA subtag registry](https://www.iana.org/assignments/language-subtag-registry/language-subtag-registry) and the language-version signal follows [Google's guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions) — details on the [Verification](/reference/verification/) page.

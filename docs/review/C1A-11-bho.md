## Register decisions

- रउआ / राउर for the host throughout the pandit's spoken lines and the step actions (brief §6.4); -ीं imperatives (लीं, राखीं, चढ़ाईं, बइठीं, छींटीं, पहिराईं, नहवाईं); third-person honorific -सु for the host couple and the purohit in the roles and notes (बइठसु, पढ़सु, करावसु, होखसु).
- First person of the pandit: हम … बानी / हम … कहीं / हम रुकब — never मैं.
- Reported speech inside the katha: the Lord addresses Narada and the old Brahmin addresses the poor Brahmin as तू / तोहरा (familiar, as the Sanskrit त्वम् / वत्स does); Narada and the poor Brahmin address the Lord as रउआ / राउर. Same choice as C1A-13; flagged for the owner there and again below.
- Term-bank bho forms used over tatsama: बरत (not व्रत), पुर्नवासी (not पूर्णिमा, except inside `[[purnima]]`), जजमान, परसाद, नहान (snan), अरघ (arghya), अछत (akshat), थरिया, नरियर, हरदी, घीव, चीनी, बियफे (Thursday), गोड़ (feet), केरा, गोहूँ, चाउर, भोर/भोरे, काल्ह, अगिला, दुसरा.
- Kept tatsama where the word is a shastric term or a deity name (ण kept): सत्यनारायण, गणेश, वरुण, नवग्रह, पुराण, षोडशोपचार, प्राणायाम, प्रदक्षिणा, पुष्पांजलि, विसर्जन, नैवेद्य, दक्षिणा, पंचामृत, पवित्रीकरण, कौस्तुभ मनि; ण→न in everyday words: गुन, निरगुन, कारन, चरन, समन, श्रवन, सुमिरन, परनाम, साधारन, बिवरन, प्रान, आयोजन, मनि, ग्रहन, विशेषन, दारसनिक.
- Nukta only in ड़/ढ़: जरूरी, रोज, जगह, जवाब, दरिद्र, नतीजा, रकम (no ज़ / फ़ / ख़).
- Mantra incipits quoted in Bhojpuri prose keep their Sanskrit spelling (यज्ञेन यज्ञमयजन्त, यान्तु देवगणाः, आगच्छ देव देवेश) — G10 for bho checks nukta only, so no change was forced; the Hindi twins had to be respelled (see the Hindi pack).
- "with" = संगे (not साथ / साथे); "or" = भा; "and" = आ; "then" = फेर; "again and again" = बेर-बेर; "only" = खाली; "by custom" = परंपरा में; "according to" = के मोताबिक.
- Honesty frame: परंपरा में मानल जाला कि … on every efficacy statement; the katha's own fruit statements are introduced as कथा आपन शब्द में कहेले / ई कथा के आपन कथन बा / फलश्रुति.
- Soft-list words avoided after the first gate run: नया → नयका (×2), पूर्व-पूजन → पहिले के पूजन (×2), पक्का → नियमित (variant) — the remaining पक्का in the adhyaya 1 retelling is Narada's own thought (ध्रुवम्) and was kept.

## Unsure about

- जजमान जोड़ी for "the host couple" — जोड़ी feels colloquial; alternatives जजमान-दंपति / जजमान आ उनकर घरवाली; owner to decide (C1A-01 asked the same about जजमान).
- घरवाली for "the wife" — vs पत्नी / मेहरारू / धर्मपत्नी in a ritual register (C1A-01 open question, repeated).
- हित-नाता for "kinsfolk" (बान्धव) — vs नाता-गोता / कुटुम / सगा-संबंधी (C1A-13 open question, repeated).
- थापना / थापित for "installation" (स्थापना) — tadbhava chosen for the action text; the Sanskrit field keeps स्थापयामि. Is थापना natural for a murti, or only for a kalash/deity in a festival?
- नहान for the deity's bath (snan-upachara) — the term bank gives नहान for snan; नहवाईं as the causative imperative ("bathe the Lord") — natural?
- औकात for "means / capacity" (यथाशक्ति, घर की सामर्थ्य) — neutral in Bhojpuri or slightly rough? Alternative: सामरथ / हैसियत / जेतना बन सके.
- मोताबिक for "according to" — Urdu-origin, common in spoken Bhojpuri; alternative: के हिसाब से / अनुसार.
- नतीजा for "outcome" and रकम for "amount" — same question (Urdu-origin everyday words vs फल / राशि).
- थम for a banana stem/trunk (केले का खंभा) — or खंभा / थंभ / गाछ?
- भुंजल for "roasted" (flour) — vs भूँजल / भुनल.
- काजर and सेनुर in the Maharashtra variant (कज्जल, सिन्दूर as offerings to the Devi) — folk spellings right here, or keep काजल / सिंदूर because the context is a listed offering?
- ठाड़ हो जाईं for "please stand" — vs खड़ा हो जाईं.
- पठाईं for "send (prasad) to the neighbours" — vs भेजीं.
- गोड़ for the deity's feet (पाद्य, चरण) — respectful enough for a deity, or should it be चरन?
- विशेषन for "epithet" (विशेषण with ण→न) — or the tatsama विशेषण since it is a grammatical term?
- दारसनिक for "philosophical" (दार्शनिक) — or keep दार्शनिक as a learned word?
- मिलावट for "mixture" (panjiri) — in Hindi मिलावट means adulteration; in Bhojpuri is it neutral? Alternative: मिश्रन / घोर.
- सुमिरन for "remembrance" (स्मरण) — vs स्मरन.
- Deity for the Lord speaking to Narada / the old Brahmin speaking to the poor Brahmin: तू / तोहरा (familiar) — or आप-style रउआ even downward? (C1A-13 Q repeated.)
- "Satyanarayan Bhagavan ki jai" → सत्यनारायण भगवान के जय — is के right in the jai-call, or does Bhojpuri keep the Hindi की here as a fixed phrase? (की is on the hard list, so के was used.)
- बियफे for Thursday (term bank) — the owner's region may say बिफे / बृहस्पतवार.
- एक-दू ब्राह्मण — vs एक-दुइ.
- पियास (thirst) — vs पिआस.
- सवा माप — is माप the natural word (vs नाप / मात्रा)?

## Register decisions (Hindi)
- Standard Khari Boli; anusvara for the pancham varna in all prose (पंडित, संकल्प, मंत्र, कंकण, मंडप, निमंत्रण); conjunct forms only inside quoted Sanskrit, and even those were lifted out of Hindi prose where the gate's G10 scan would see them (locators/paraphrases instead).
- Nukta: ज़्यादा, ज़रूरत kept where used; no क़/ग़; तारीख़ not needed here.
- कन्या / वर / वधू in the ritual sense; दूल्हा only in the quoted folk phrase "दूल्हा राजा"; माता-पिता, बुआ, नाई-नाइन as the family words.
- मड़वा and मंडप both appear: मंडप in the Hindi prose and the paddhati headings, मड़वा where the Bhojpuri custom (मड़वा मटकोर) is named; the owner may want one spelling in Hindi prose.
- Latin digits everywhere (VS 13.20, 1.3.21, 108); Devanagari digits only inside the Devanagari fields where the source has them (none here — verse numbers stripped per CONTENT-RULES §4).
- Honesty: "परंपरा में माना जाता है" on the two tradition statements (earth's leave; the groom as Narayana-form); no outcome claims; sensitive[] not set (no health/grief/pregnancy/astrology content).

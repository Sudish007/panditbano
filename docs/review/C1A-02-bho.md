## Register decisions
- जजमान (not यजमान) in all bho prose, per the R5 §7 fallback (hi यजमान / bho जजमान); the glossary mark [[yajman]] renders the bho display form.
- दक्खिना for dakshina in bho prose (ण→न rule, tadbhava), but the glossary term [[dakshina]] keeps its bank spelling; owner to confirm दक्खिना vs दक्षिणा.
- असौच for अशौच (श→स in everyday Bhojpuri, as सुद्ध for शुद्ध); the Sanskrit-quoting fields keep अशौच. Owner to confirm.
- सूतक and पातक kept as tatsama (the terms every Bhojpuri household uses); मृतासौच/जननासौच given once as glosses.
- नछत्र for नक्षत्र, पच्छ for पक्ष, करन for करण, महीना for मास in running prose; inside the sankalpa table the Sanskrit locatives (नक्षत्रे, पक्षे, करणे) are left as spoken forms.
- मौत for मृत्यु (term bank: mrityu = मौत), जनम for जन्म, बियाह for विवाह, बरत for व्रत, नाँव for नाम, देस for देश, सहर for शहर, बिदेस for विदेश, जगह for स्थान.
- महतारी / बाबूजी for mother / father (term bank), घरवाली for the host's wife, मरद for husband, मेहरारू for woman, लइका/बचवा for child.
- Imperatives in -ीं throughout (लीं, दीं, देखीं, राखीं, दोहराईं, बोलीं, करीं); the pandit's own line uses बानी/हम … करब.
- ओकरा बाद / एकरा बाद (never उसके बाद); एही से for इसलिए; बाकिर for लेकिन; फेर for फिर; संगे for साथ; खातिर for के लिए; भा for या; चाहे … चाहे for या … या.
- Honesty frame: परंपरा में मानल जाला (never माना जाता है); "tradition gives as the reason" → परंपरा में एकर कारन ई बतावल जाला.
- Soft-list words left deliberately: नया (the term bank has no attested Bhojpuri form; नवका/नयका would be dialectal), विवाह/व्रत inside quoted Sanskrit titles ('व्रतं करिष्ये') and the Dwivedi book title.
- The worked example's Sanskrit is quoted identically in all three languages (it is the mantra text, not prose); only the Hindi page swaps conjuncts for the anusvara where the gate requires it.

## Unsure about
- दक्खिना vs दक्षिणा — tadbhava vs tatsama in prose; owner to decide.
- असौच vs अशौच — same question.
- जजमान vs यजमान — R5 §7 item 7 fallback used (जजमान).
- घरवाली vs पत्नी vs मेहरारू for "the host's wife" (also open from C1A-01).
- सोग vs शोक for grief.
- तुरते for "immediately" (also तुरंत); बिसम for विषम (odd number) — attested? 
- बीया (seed) for "the seed of the later ekadashah" — owner to confirm the spelling (बिया is the term-bank fallback).
- गोरू/झोरा/थरिया/पट्टीदार — regional words used once each; owner to confirm they read naturally.
- Weekday/month forms: एतवार…सनिचर and चइत…फागुन used in the reference table; the Sanskrit vāsara forms (शनिवासरे) left as spoken Sanskrit.
- 'पंडित के शिष्टाचार-पोथी' as the bho title of F16 — or 'पंडित के सहूर-सलीका'? owner's ear.
- 'उद्देश्य' kept (no everyday Bhojpuri word found; मकसद is Urdu-origin and used in the fixture).
- 'एगारहवाँ', 'बारहवाँ', 'तेरहवाँ' ordinals — Shubhvela-style spellings assumed.

## Inferences phrased as practice (flagged for the owner)

- "Many urban families today keep the sutak for three days or until the chhathi" (F17 step 7) — present practice, no survey; phrased as observation.

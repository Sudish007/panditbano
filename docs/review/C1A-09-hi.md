## Register decisions
- Standard Khari Boli; anusvara for the pancham varna (संकल्प, मंत्र, अंतःपट, गंधर्व, इंद्र, चंद्र, संमुखीकरण, संमार्जन); Sanskrit incipits with nasal conjuncts are not quoted inside Hindi prose (G10) — the mantra records carry the Devanagari.
- Hindi forms vs Sanskrit: गठबंधन / ग्रंथि-बंधन, भाँवर (not फेरा) in the step titles, पाँव-पूजी (the pothi's पौंपुजी), लावा/खील, सिल for the ashmarohana stone.
- The bride is कन्या in the kanyadaan context and वधू from the laja-homa onward (as the sutra switches); the groom is वर throughout.
- Honesty frames on every promised fruit: "परंपरा में माना जाता है", "श्लोक का अपना कथन", "यह साइट उसे ग्रंथ का कथन मानकर देती है"; the godan sankalpa's "दोष-निरसन" is framed as tradition.
- Gender-sensitive content (the groom's vows) is presented as the pothi's text with a note that families may omit, balance or rephrase — no editorialising beyond that (R4 §5 factual handling).

## Register decisions
- Standard Khari Boli; anusvara for the pancham varna everywhere in prose (पंडित, मंत्र, संध्या, अंत्येष्टि, चिंतामणि, धर्मसिंधु, स्कंद पुराण, रेवा खंड, माध्यंदिन, संपूर्णानंद, केंद्रीय, उत्तराखंड, रामानंदाचार्य) — the registry's conjunct spellings (अन्त्यकर्म, स्कन्दपुराण) are not used in Hindi prose; new registry titles were added in the anusvara form so the G16 link rule matches the Hindi text.
- Nukta: ज़/फ़ where pronounced (ज़रूरत, ज़्यादा, फ़ोन, फ़ॉन्ट, फ़ाइल, ब्राउज़र, डिज़ाइन, आवाज़, ऑफ़लाइन, दस्तावेज़, हवाई-जहाज़); ख़ only in तारीख़; no क़/ग़ (नुक्ता, नकल, कानून).
- "page" = पृष्ठ; "record" = अभिलेख; "checklist" = सामग्री-सूची; "key" (storage) = कुंजी; "setting" = सेटिंग; "service worker" = सर्विस वर्कर; "cache storage" = कैश संग्रह; "sitemap" = साइट-मानचित्र; "release" = प्रकाशन; "report" = रिपोर्ट.
- Honesty frames: "परंपरा में माना जाता है", "प्रचलन में", "लोक-रीति है"; no fruit claims; the lint words (दूर करता है, ठीक करता है, गारंटी, सौ प्रतिशत, गर्भ) were avoided in the site's own voice — the upanayana sentence was reworded to avoid गर्भ.
- Sanskrit sutras quoted verbatim (PGS 1.4.5, 1.17.1, 1.17.5, 1.19.1, 2.1.1–2, 3.4.2) with a Hindi paraphrase after the dash.
- Deities and planets in the respectful plural where they are subjects (बृहस्पति या शुक्र … हों; चंद्र … हो/हों per sentence).
- Term-bank marks on first use in each file: [[vidhi]], [[mantra]], [[katha]], [[sanskar]], [[puja]], [[vrat]], [[muhurat]], [[samagri]], [[pandit]], [[svara]], [[lipyantaran]], [[padachheda]], [[vinyog]], [[yajman]], [[pothi]], [[paddhati]], [[kalash-sthapana]], [[nandi-shraddha]], [[panchang]], [[nakshatra]], [[tithi]], [[lagna]], [[upanayan]], [[grih-pravesh]], [[mundan]], [[kharmas]], [[sankranti]], [[chaturmas]], [[devshayani-ekadashi]], [[dev-uthani-ekadashi]], [[adhik-maas]], [[pitru-paksha]], [[shraddha]], [[rahu-kaal]], [[bhadra]], [[panchak]], [[uttarayan]], [[shukla-paksha]], [[vivah]], [[sagai]], [[namkaran]], [[nishkraman]], [[annaprashan]], [[vidyarambh]], [[janma-tithi]]. Hindi first-use alternates come from the term bank (विवाह (शादी), अधिक मास (मलमास), सगाई (रोका), उपनयन (जनेऊ संस्कार), विद्यारंभ (अक्षरारंभ), यजमान (जजमान)).
